## Interpretation Warnings

- **Ratings are not exploitability.** They reflect performance against the specific suite pool used, not how exploitable a policy is in general.
- **High RD means uncertain.** A large rating deviation indicates the estimate is based on few games or inconsistent results. Do not treat high-RD ratings as precise.
- **Do not compare ratings across different suite pools** unless the report says they share a source. Different suites use different matchup compositions and opponent sets.
- **Ratings are research-local and not product difficulty.** They measure relative policy strength within a fixed benchmark context, not a calibrated difficulty tier for players.
