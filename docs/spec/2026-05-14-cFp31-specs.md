## Purpose

Calibrate `legal-heuristic-v1` so it no longer overcommits Round 1 and leaves
Round 2 / Round 3 as formalities because it has exhausted useful cards.

cFp24.2 and cFp28 made v1 safer about passing when the opponent still has many
cards. That fixed premature passing, but playtests now show the opposite
failure mode: v1 keeps taking every positive move in Round 1 until it has no
meaningful future hand. cFp31 adds a narrow round-investment layer that can
choose to preserve the hand or sacrifice a non-elimination round.

## Current Failure Pattern

Recent product diagnostics show this trend:

- v1 refuses to voluntarily pass unless its lead exceeds the current
  hand-pressure threshold.
- If it is not safely ahead, it keeps selecting the best useful play.
- Once its remaining hand has no positive unit/hero tempo, it passes Round 2
  and Round 3 because passing is then rational.

This is not a weather-placement bug, not a Medic-timing bug, and not a simple
pass-threshold bug. cFp30 and cFp29 are working in the observed lines. The
missing layer is round economy:

> Is the current round worth spending the remaining useful future hand?

## Goals

1. Add hidden-info-safe `roundInvestmentAnalysis` diagnostics to v1 decision
   traces.
2. Let v1 pass or sacrifice a non-elimination round when continuing would burn
   the last useful future hand for insufficient immediate gain.
3. Preserve cFp30 weather-aware placement, cFp29 Medic timing, cFp28 hand-shape
   diagnostics, cFp27 mulligan diagnostics, and cFp26 pass diagnostics.
4. Keep last-gem behavior aggressive: on the last gem, v1 should still fight
   unless the existing upper-bound gate says catch-up is impossible.
5. Update v1 docs, AI Lab/product policy metadata, benchmark artifacts, tests,
   project state, and report.

## Hidden-Info Boundary

Use only `EnginePolicyInput`:

- `seatId`
- `SeatObservation`
- legal moves

Do not infer from command/event history. Do not add command history to
`SeatObservation`. Do not use battle-log data. Do not expose AI hand card
names, source IDs, instance IDs, linked source IDs, or ability arrays in
diagnostic exports.

Important: exact "cards spent this round" is not available from
`SeatObservation` without command history. cFp31 should therefore use
observation-derived public commitment proxies:

- own board unit/hero/card counts this round;
- own/opponent hand counts;
- own/opponent gem counts;
- current round number;
- positive future move counts from legal-move scoring;
- estimated after-move hand/future-risk buckets.

Do **not** fabricate exact historical spend counts.

## Required Implementation

### 1. Add Round-Investment Diagnostic Types

Update `src/game/ai/decisionTrace.ts`.

Add:

```ts
export type AiDecisionRoundInvestmentRisk =
  | "none"
  | "watch"
  | "high"
  | "critical";

export type AiDecisionRoundInvestmentRecommendation =
  | "none"
  | "preserve_future_hand"
  | "sacrifice_round"
  | "fight_last_gem"
  | "continue";

export interface AiDecisionRoundInvestmentAnalysis {
  readonly ownBoardUnitCount: number;
  readonly ownBoardHeroCount: number;
  readonly ownBoardNonHeroUnitCount: number;
  readonly ownBoardHornCount: number;
  readonly ownBoardCardCount: number;
  readonly positiveFutureUnitMoveCount: number;
  readonly positiveFutureNonUnitMoveCount: number;
  readonly futureRoundHandQuality: AiDecisionHandQuality;
  readonly currentRoundHandCount: number;
  readonly estimatedHandCountAfterSelectedMove: number;
  readonly selectedMoveSpendsHandCard: boolean;
  readonly selectedMoveIsCardAdvantage: boolean;
  readonly selectedMoveWouldLeaveNoPositiveUnitMove: boolean;
  readonly selectedMoveWouldLeaveNoUnitTempoCard: boolean;
  readonly nonEliminationRound: boolean;
  readonly scoreDelta: number;
  readonly risk: AiDecisionRoundInvestmentRisk;
  readonly recommendation: AiDecisionRoundInvestmentRecommendation;
}
```

Notes:

- `ownBoardCardCount` should be the sum of own board units/heroes plus own row
  horns. Weather cards are global/public but not "own board investment" for
  this diagnostic.
- `positiveFutureUnitMoveCount` and `positiveFutureNonUnitMoveCount` should
  align with existing cFp28 scored-candidate semantics.
- `estimatedHandCountAfterSelectedMove` is an approximation. For `play_card`,
  start with `ownHandCount - 1`; for obvious Spy plays, add up to two draws
  bounded by `ownDeckCount`; for non-hand moves, leave hand count unchanged.
  Do not reveal or assume drawn card identities.
- `selectedMoveWouldLeaveNoPositiveUnitMove` may be approximated from current
  positive unit move count and whether the selected move is a positive unit/hero
  move. It does not need perfect post-state simulation.
- `selectedMoveWouldLeaveNoUnitTempoCard` may be approximated from
  `unitCardCount + heroCardCount` and whether the selected hand card is a
  unit/hero. It does not need to model Muster/draw side effects beyond the Spy
  hand-count exception above.

Add `roundInvestmentAnalysis: AiDecisionRoundInvestmentAnalysis | null` to
`AiDecisionTrace`.

### 3. Round-Investment Behavior Rules

Integrate the new decision gate in `choosePlayingMove(features)`.

The intended shape:

1. Existing prompt, mulligan, round-end, opponent-passed, and last-gem
   impossible branches remain first.
2. Existing voluntary safe-pass behavior remains.
3. Before returning `bestUsefulMove(features)` in active play, evaluate whether
   a legal pass should be chosen to preserve future hand.

The new pass gate should only apply when:

- `passMove` exists;
- opponent has not passed;
- `ownGems > 1` (non-elimination round);
- there is no prompt pending;
- phase is normal playing through the existing `choosePlayingMove` path;
- candidate is not clearly card-advantage-positive (Spy or similarly obvious
  draw-positive hand-count move);
- the policy is not already safely passing through cFp28/cFp24.2 logic.

The pass gate should recommend pass when at least one of these applies:

#### A. Preserve Future Hand While Ahead Or Near Even

The AI is ahead or close enough that continuing is optional:

- `scoreDelta >= -10`
- and continuing with the best useful move would leave:
  - no positive unit/hero future moves, or
  - no unit/hero tempo card in hand, or
  - hand quality `poor` / `thin` with `estimatedHandCountAfterSelectedMove <= 2`

This covers Round 1 / Round 2 overcommit where v1 has a playable positive move
but should accept that the opponent may take the round cheaply.

#### B. Sacrifice A Non-Elimination Round When Catch-Up Is Too Expensive

The AI is behind on a non-elimination round and the best useful move is not a
single clean catch-up:

- `scoreDelta < 0`
- `ownGems > 1`
- no single-move catch-up from existing `buildLegalHeuristicV1PassDecisionDiagnostics`
  OR the best candidate would leave a critical future-hand risk

In this case, pass to preserve cards rather than spending down into a likely
lost round.

#### C. Critical Future-Hand Risk

Regardless of score sign, if:

- `estimatedHandCountAfterSelectedMove <= 1`
- and `selectedMoveWouldLeaveNoPositiveUnitMove === true`
- and `ownGems > 1`

then prefer pass unless selected move is card-advantage-positive or wins the
match. For this heuristic, "wins the match" only needs to mean:

- opponent has `opponentGems <= 1`;
- and the candidate's estimated tempo reaches `minimumScoreToWinRound(features)`.

Do not add deeper game-end lookahead.

### 4. Do Not Overcorrect

The following must remain true:

- Last gem still fights unless catch-up is impossible by existing upper-bound
  logic.
- If opponent has passed and AI is ahead, pass.
- If opponent has passed and AI can catch up cheaply, existing catch-up behavior
  should remain.
- Spy/card-advantage moves can still be played in Round 1 even when hand is
  low, because they preserve or improve future hand count.
- cFp30 weather-aware placement remains active.
- cFp29 Medic timing remains active.
- cFp28 pass safety buffer remains active.
