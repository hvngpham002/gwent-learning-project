   `102/16/2 -> 89/29/2` starter-matrix regression.

   - cFp35 count.
   - repaired cFp36 count.

5. Failure-mining deltas by tuning queue cluster:
   - rank, cluster ID, count, and recommendation.
6. Interpretation:
   - explain whether the regression appears primarily caused by
     round-resource pass behavior, matchup/deck skew, weathered-row placement,
     Medic timing, or analyzer noise.

## Initial Hypothesis

The repaired cFp36 gate is mechanically correct but too blunt. The likely next
phase is a narrower cFp37 pass/round-investment alignment phase rather than
another broad budget change. This hypothesis must be confirmed or rejected by
the artifact comparison.

## Revision Notes

Iteration 1 confirms the initial hypothesis with a sharper conclusion:

- cFp36 improved the narrow `round_one_overinvestment` finding from `10` to `5`.
- Total starter-matrix strength regressed from `102/16/2` to `89/29/2`.
- Failure-mining findings rose from `295` to `397`.
- `suspicious_pass` rose from `222` to `318`.
- New suspicious-pass findings frequently have
  `roundInvestmentRecommendation === "continue"` or `"fight_last_gem"`.

The next phase should not tune resource-budget constants directly. It should be
`cFp37` Pass Decision Alignment / Resource Gate Scope-Down: preserve the
diagnostic fields, but stop the cFp36 gate from overriding existing continue,
last-gem fight, and single-move catch-up paths unless a narrower explicit pass
reason exists.
