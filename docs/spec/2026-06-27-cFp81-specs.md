## Decision

Create a new casebook artifact family over committed cFp80 artifacts instead of
modifying cFp80:

- cFp80 remains the immutable compact feature source.
- cFp81 consumes cFp80 dictionaries, root rows, compact action rows, skipped
  rows, and summaries.
- cFp81 emits scalar aggregate summaries and bounded casebook rows.
- cFp81 may reconstruct public/scalar action-feature fields in memory for
  grouping, but it must not write full reconstructed cFp79 action rows.
- cFp81 must keep every output hidden-info-safe and below artifact size limits.

The casebook should be descriptive, not prescriptive:

- count coverage;
- classify feature-surface slices;
- identify high-collision / high-branching / over-budget / skipped-root
  pockets;
- report readiness and residual gaps;
- recommend a next benchmark-only phase.

Do not compute action values, rank actions, choose actions, or infer strength.

## Readiness Classification

cFp81 should not rank actions. It may classify feature-surface slices as
consumer-readiness signals.

Use deterministic scalar rules such as:

- `consumer_ready`: ordinary slice with enough root/action coverage and no
  elevated collision/branching/cap/skipped concentration.
- `consumer_ready_high_collision`: slice has a high share of actions with
  `hasCollision === true` or large `moveCountBucket`.
- `consumer_ready_high_branching`: slice is associated with high cFp72
  branching risk buckets.
- `consumer_ready_over_budget_context`: slice is associated with cFp74
  over-budget or cap-pressure context.
- `consumer_ready_sparse_slice`: slice has low action/root count and should not
  be overinterpreted.
- `consumer_ready_skipped_root_context`: suite/slice has nonzero inherited
  skipped-root concentration that must be reported beside future consumer
  results.

The exact thresholds are implementation choices, but they must be:

- deterministic;
- documented in `summary.json` and `report.md`;
- tested with focused fixtures;
- not framed as gameplay strength or action quality.
