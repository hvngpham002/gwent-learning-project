# Cluster C Phase 26 — Draw Extra Card Setup Leader Implementation Report

- **Spec:** `docs/spec/2026-05-03-cCp26-specs.md`

- **Branch:** `dev`
- **Outcome:** Implemented. Tranche 3 of `docs/leader-ability-matrix.md` is in progress (1 of 2 members landed).

## Summary

Promotes `draw_extra_card` for
`scoiatael.francesca-findabair-daisy-of-the-valley` (Francesca
Findabair: Daisy of the Valley) from `placeholder` to `implemented`
as a **setup-time initial hand-size modifier**, opening Tranche 3 of
the leader ability matrix.

While this leader is the seat's leader, the seat draws **11** cards
(not 10) from the top of the already-shuffled deck during `startMatch`
initial draw, before mulligan opens. The mulligan budget remains
unchanged at two redraws. The leader emits no `use_leader` legal
move, never sets `seat.leaderUsed`, and never emits `leader_used`.
The 11th card is the next deterministic card off the seeded-RNG-
shuffled deck top — there is no prompt, no card-by-card choice, and
no faction/strength/row filtering.

A new manifest bucket `implementedSetupLeaderSourceIds` is introduced
to record setup-time leaders distinctly from passive scoring policies
(`implementedPassiveLeaderSourceIds`) and active executable leaders
(`executableLeaderSourceIds`). `implementedLeaderSourceIds` becomes
the sorted union of all three.

## Behavior Changed

### Engine

- `startMatch` now derives the initial hand-draw count per seat from
  the new pure helper `getInitialHandDrawCountForLeader`. The Daisy
  seat draws 11 cards from the top of the already-shuffled deck
  before mulligan opens; the deck shrinks by 11 instead of 10. All
  other leaders draw the base 10 unchanged.
- The 11th card is the next deterministic card off the seeded-RNG
  shuffle. There is no prompt, no card-kind / faction / strength / row
  / ability filter — the 11th card is whatever the deterministic
  shuffle put on top after the first 10 were taken.
- `Math.random` is **not** introduced. The new helper does not advance
  RNG; `setup.ts` still uses the existing `createSeededRng(seed)` +
  `shuffleWithRng` flow once per seat.
- For a given seed and deck list, the Daisy seat's setup is byte-for-
  byte deterministic. When two configs differ only in leader
  identity, Daisy's first 10 hand cards exactly match the equivalent
  non-Daisy seat's first 10 hand cards; Daisy's 11th hand card is the
  next card that would otherwise have remained on top of the
  non-Daisy seat's deck.
- The mulligan budget remains capped at two redraws. Legal mulligan
  moves naturally include 1 keep-hand + 11 single-card mulligan
  options on the first mulligan tick (12 total) for the Daisy seat.
  After two redraws, the seat's `mulliganComplete` flips to true and
  emits no further legal mulligan moves, identical to the existing
  10-card flow.
- The leader emits no `use_leader` legal move at any phase, never sets
  `seat.leaderUsed`, and never emits `leader_used`. Manual `UseLeader`
  attempts continue to reject through the existing unsupported-leader
  path (`assertLegal` → `EngineRuleError`) without state mutation.
- Robustness on tiny decks: a malformed or test deck shorter than 11
  cards does not throw solely because of `draw_extra_card`; the
  existing `slice` behavior draws available cards (e.g. a 4-card
  deck under Daisy yields a 4-card hand and an empty deck).
- Setup events reuse existing types: `card_moved` with
  `reason === "initial_draw"` for each drawn card (11 events for
  Daisy), and `initial_hand_drawn` with `cardIds.length === 11` for
  the Daisy seat. No new event types.

### Manifest classification

- Introduces a new readonly bucket `implementedSetupLeaderSourceIds`
  on the `OfficialLeaderPromotionManifest` interface. Currently 1
  entry: `scoiatael.francesca-findabair-daisy-of-the-valley`.
- `executableLeaderSourceIds` is unchanged at 11.
- `implementedPassiveLeaderSourceIds` is unchanged at 7.
- `implementedLeaderSourceIds` becomes the sorted union of all three
  (11 + 7 + 1 = 19).
- `placeholderLeaderAbilityIds` shrinks from 4 to 3 (removes
  `draw_extra_card`), leaving `cancel_leader`,
  `discard_two_draw_one_from_deck`, `look_three_cards`.

### Documentation

- `docs/gwent-rules.md` §17.12k is the new authoritative section for
  the setup-time hand-size modifier contract.
- `docs/leader-ability-matrix.md` Pattern 7 is now the durable
  reference for the Setup-Time Hand-Size Modifier pattern.

None.

`src/game/core/leaderSetup.ts` rather than embedding string checks in
`setup.ts`; the implementation creates exactly that module with the
suggested exports
(`DRAW_EXTRA_CARD_LEADER_SOURCE_ID`, `BASE_INITIAL_HAND_SIZE`,
`getInitialHandDrawCountForLeader`).

```text

implementedLeaderSourceIds: 19

```

Implementation matches exactly.

The spec asked the helper to look up the leader by source ID and
return `baseDrawCount + 1` only when the resolved leader's ability is
`draw_extra_card`; the implementation matches with a defensive
synthetic-catalog test confirming the helper checks `ability` rather
than just trusting the source ID.

## Risks / Follow-ups

- **Setup-time bucket is currently a one-entry set.** The new
  `implementedSetupLeaderSourceIds` bucket exists for shape and
  classification clarity; it is not motivated by a second pending
  setup-time leader. If a future leader is added with a setup-time
  effect, the bucket already exists; if not, the bucket remains a
  one-entry set indefinitely. This is the correct trade-off per the
  spec: setup-time leaders are conceptually different from passive
  scoring policies and should not be conflated.
- **Helper does not advance RNG.** The 11th card comes from the
  existing seeded-RNG shuffle; no extra RNG advancement is performed
  for `draw_extra_card`. If a future setup-time leader needs random
  selection (e.g. "draw 1 random card from deck excluding heroes"),
  it must thread RNG explicitly through the helper or compute its
  selection in `setup.ts` after the helper returns the count.
- **Mulligan UI on the 11th card.** The mulligan UI naturally
  renders 11 cards plus the existing keep-hand and one-card mulligan
  options, but layout/spacing was not exercised in this phase
  (browser smoke is skipped). If a future Cluster E pass enforces a
  fixed-width mulligan grid, the 11-card hand may need a layout
  adjustment.
- **Tiny test decks under Daisy.** The implementation does not
  throw if a test deck has fewer than 11 cards; the existing `slice`
  behavior draws available cards. Production decks always have
  enough cards for an 11-card initial draw, so this is purely a
  testing safety net.
- **AI heuristic value.** `legal-heuristic-v0` does not weight Daisy
  differently from other Scoia'tael leaders. This is correct because
  the heuristic does not factor leader identity into card selection;
  it only ranks legal moves by their immediate effect on the score.
  A future heuristic upgrade could weight Daisy's extra-card
  advantage explicitly.

## Recommended Next Step

cCp26 lands the first Tranche 3 member. The recommended next phase
is **cCp27 — `discard_two_draw_one_from_deck` multi-stage leader
prompt** on Eredin: Destroyer of Worlds. Per settled product decision
§4: discard up to two cards from hand, then show all remaining deck
cards, choose any one card to draw, then shuffle the remaining deck.
Implementable with at least one card in hand; zero cards in hand
makes it unusable. Hidden-info redaction must keep deck identities
visible only to the acting seat.

This is the engine's first multi-stage prompt and is non-trivial; it
will likely require extending `PendingPrompt` to carry stage state
or chaining two single-step prompts. After cCp27, only Tranche 4
(hidden-info / cancel) remains.
