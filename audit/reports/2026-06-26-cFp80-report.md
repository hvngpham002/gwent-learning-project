## Behavior Changed

cFp80 adds a new benchmark-only artifact family:

```text
search-consumer-action-features-dictionary-v0/cFp80
```

It reads committed cFp79 artifacts, validates cFp79 readiness and row counts,
builds deterministic dictionaries for repeated action-feature strings, writes
one compact action row per cFp79 action row, forwards root/skipped rows with
cFp80 metadata, and proves reconstruction for preserved public/scalar cFp79
action fields.

cFp80 does not:

- modify cFp79, cFp76, or cFp78 artifact directories;
- execute candidate actions;
- rebuild sampled states;
- materialize hidden worlds;
- re-observe benchmark roots;
- run rollout search, PIMC evaluation, or ISMCTS/MCTS;
- compute action values, expected values, reward targets, win probabilities,
  payoff tables, or principal variations;
- rank actions or choose best actions;
- change gameplay behavior, engine rules, legal moves, AI policy behavior,
  sampler behavior, deck/catalog data, benchmark suite definitions, or rating
  artifacts;
- expose AI Lab runner buttons or wire product search AI.

The only product-surface change is static AI Lab metadata pointing at cFp80 as
feature compaction infrastructure.

## Deviations

No scope deviations. cFp80 uses plain JSON/JSONL dictionary projection and
declares compact action row schema/run constants once in `manifest.json` and
`summary.json`, rather than repeating those constants in every compact action
row. This follows the spec's row-constant compaction direction and is what
keeps robust `action-features-compact.jsonl` below the 50 MB target.
