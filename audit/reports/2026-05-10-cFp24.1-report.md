## Behavior Changed

- Adds `PRODUCT_AI_POLICIES`, `DEFAULT_PRODUCT_AI_POLICY_ID`,
  `resolveProductAiPolicyId(...)`, `getProductAiPolicy(...)`, and
  `aiPolicyIdFromSearch(...)`.
- Pre-game renders an `AI policy` selector with:
  - `stable · legal-heuristic-v0`
  - `experimental · legal-heuristic-v1`
- Setup config, `StartEngineMatchOptions`, and `EngineAdapterState` now
  carry `aiPolicyId`.
- `getLegalHeuristicAiCommand(...)` resolves the active product policy
  from adapter state instead of calling v0 directly.
- Pre-game summary, casual mode note, mulligan seed block, match topbar,
  and AI-turn next-action copy show the selected policy id.
- `/ai-lab` builds product policy rows from shared product metadata and
  keeps `legal-first-v0` as a separate benchmark-only row.

## URL Contract

- `/` -> `legal-heuristic-v0`
- `/?ai=legal-heuristic-v0` -> `legal-heuristic-v0`
- `/?ai=legal-heuristic-v1` -> `legal-heuristic-v1`
- `/match` -> `legal-heuristic-v0`
- `/match?ai=legal-heuristic-v1` -> `legal-heuristic-v1`
- `/?engine=1&ui=authentic&ai=legal-heuristic-v1` -> pre-game with v1
  selected
- `/?ai=legal-first-v0` -> `legal-heuristic-v0`
- `/?ai=unknown` -> `legal-heuristic-v0`

Only exact policy ids are accepted. No abbreviated aliases were added.

## Selector Behavior

The selector is labelled `AI policy`, not difficulty. It initializes from
the `ai` query param, defaults invalid or absent values to v0, updates
the setup config used by `begin match`, and does not update the URL.

## Defaults And Selection

v0 remains default through `DEFAULT_PRODUCT_AI_POLICY_ID`, initial
adapter state, `startEngineMatch()` without `aiPolicyId`, `/`, and
`/match`.

v1 is selected only by the pre-game selector, `?ai=legal-heuristic-v1`,
or an explicit `startEngineMatch({ aiPolicyId: "legal-heuristic-v1" })`
call.

## Benchmark-Only Boundary

`legal-first-v0` is not in `PRODUCT_AI_POLICY_IDS`, is rejected by
`resolveProductAiPolicyId(...)`, and is not rendered as a pre-game
option. It remains visible only in AI Lab as a benchmark-only comparator.

## AI Lab Freshness

AI Lab product policy rows are derived from `PRODUCT_AI_POLICIES`. This
prevents registered product policies from drifting into stale local
status strings. `legal-heuristic-v1` now appears as implemented and
experimental/playtest; no product policy row is labelled not implemented.

## Hidden-Info Safety Notes

The selected policy id is public metadata. The controller still builds
policy input through `buildSeatObservation(...)` and legal moves, so v1
receives the same hidden-info-safe observation boundary as benchmarks.
No UI path exposes AI hand or deck identities, and AI Lab still avoids
benchmark runtime imports and unsafe output.
