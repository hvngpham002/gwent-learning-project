overinvestment — the behavior-actionable signal from the cFp42 casebook. The
guard reduces cases where v1 spends too many hand cards in round 1, enters

## Behavior Changed

cFp43 adds a narrow round-one overinvestment guard to `legal-heuristic-v1`.
The guard can choose `pass` over a useful `play_card` move only when:

1. `features.round === 1`
2. `features.ownGems > 1`
3. `features.passMove` exists
4. opponent has not passed
5. candidate is `play_card`, spends a hand card, is not card advantage
6. `boardAfterSelectedMove >= 7` AND `handCountAfterSelectedMove <= 4`
7. no tactical exception applies

Tactical exceptions (allow play instead of pass):

- non-play-card candidate (`use_leader` or free action)
- own last gem (`ownGems <= 1`)
- opponent already passed
- card advantage move (`selectedMoveIsCardAdvantage === true`, e.g. Spy)
- match-winning play (opponentGems <= 1 and candidate reaches minimum score)
- cheap single-move catch-up (behind, candidate wins round, overkill <= 3)
- single-card hand (`ownHandCount <= 1`)

The guard does NOT affect:

- `legal-heuristic-v0` behavior
- rounds 2 and 3
- engine rules, legal moves, scoring, command execution
- UI behavior, catalog data, deck presets
- benchmark suite shape, ratings, search, training, product difficulty

New trace fields added to `AiDecisionRoundInvestmentAnalysis`:

```ts
roundOneBoardAfterSelectedMove: number;
roundOneOverinvestmentRecommended: boolean;
roundOneOverinvestmentReason: AiDecisionRoundOneOverinvestmentReason;
```

`AiDecisionRoundOneOverinvestmentReason` is a hidden-info-safe enum of
lower_snake_case strings (9 reason variants + "none").

## Risks And Follow-Ups

- The guard thresholds (`board >= 7`, `hand <= 4`) are conservative starting
  points. If tests show the guard is too weak to catch cFp42 casebook fixtures,
  adjust only after recording the reason in a follow-up report.
- The guard is round-1-only by design. If round-2 or round-3 overinvestment
  becomes behavior-actionable from future failure-mining analysis, that requires
  a separate spec and implementation.
- The cFp42 casebook was based on starter-deck v1-vs-v0 artifacts. Competitive
  and mechanics decks may produce different resource-pressure patterns worth
  validating in a future expanded failure-mining cycle.
- `round_three_low_resource` remains 86 findings in the expanded suite (down
  from 97). This should be monitored but not drive direct round-3 pass tuning.
