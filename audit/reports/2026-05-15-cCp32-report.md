## Behavior Changed

- **Engine:** `startMatch` now checks for exactly one Scoia'tael seat. When found, the starter is determined by `scoiataelFirstPlayerChoice` config or falls back to the Scoia'tael seat itself. `turn_set.reason` changes from `"initial_roll"` to `"scoiatael_override"`, and a `faction_ability_resolved` event with `ability: "scoiatael_choose_first"` is emitted.
- **Both Scoia'tael:** Falls back to seeded `initial_roll` (no scoiatael ability event).
- **Non-Scoia'tael:** Unchanged seeded `initial_roll` behavior.
- **Product UI:** Scoia'tael human vs non-Scoia'tael opponent now shows a "First turn" selector (you/opponent) in the pre-game screen.
