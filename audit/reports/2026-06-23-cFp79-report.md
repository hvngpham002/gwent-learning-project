## Behavior Changed

cFp79 adds a new benchmark-only artifact family:

```text
search-consumer-action-features-v1/cFp79
```

It reads committed cFp76 root features and cFp78 compact public action
identities, validates source consistency, joins by public root identity through
cFp78 `rootRef`, and writes normalized root-feature and action-feature rows.

cFp79 does not:

- modify cFp76 or cFp78 artifact directories;
- execute candidate actions;
- rebuild sampled states;
- materialize hidden worlds;
- re-observe benchmark roots;
- run rollout search, PIMC evaluation, or ISMCTS/MCTS;
- compute action values, expected values, reward targets, win probabilities,
  payoff tables, or principal variations;
- rank actions or choose best actions;
- change gameplay behavior, engine rules, legal moves, AI policy behavior,
  sampler behavior, deck/catalog data, benchmark suite definitions, or rating
  artifacts;
- expose AI Lab runner buttons or wire product search AI.

The only product-surface change is static AI Lab metadata pointing at cFp79 as
feature-join infrastructure.

## Deviations

No scope deviations. The only notable result is size-related: robust
`action-features.jsonl` is 86,029,163 bytes, above the 50 MB soft target but
below the 90 MB hard stop. The reason is documented above, and the cFp80
recommendation is compaction or dictionary encoding before consumer casebook
work.

---

## Risks And Follow-Ups

- cFp79 proves the feature join is complete and hidden-info-safe, but the
  robust action-feature file is too large for the 50 MB soft target.
- cFp80 should not jump to ranking, rollout, value, best-action, win
  probability, reward-target, search gameplay, or product AI work.
- cFp80 should first compact repeated per-row schema/readiness/join fields or
  define a deterministic subset over cFp79 action features.

## Recommended Next Step

cFp80 should be a benchmark-only compaction or dictionary-encoding phase over
cFp79 action features before any action-feature casebook or consumer-readiness
report. It must remain non-gameplay and must not add rollout, value, ranking,
best-action, win-probability, reward-target, search gameplay, or product AI.
