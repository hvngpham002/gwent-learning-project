## Risks And Follow-Ups

- The completed Heinrich and Silver annotation supports NFSP as a
  medium-term training-method reference, but it does not define Gwent
  observations, legal-command encoding, reward design, a simulator
  export schema, a benchmark ladder, or human-like difficulty
  calibration.
- The paper's experiments are poker-only and use a compact action
  vocabulary; Gwent adaptation still needs hidden-safe variable legal
  action candidates or masks.
- Future work should not cite this paper as evidence that generic DQN,
  policy-gradient, or AlphaZero-style self-play converges in
  imperfect-information Gwent.
- A faithful Gwent NFSP experiment still needs a low-compute reduced
  environment, replay/storage budget, fixed baselines, and
  exploitability or approximate-best-response probes.
