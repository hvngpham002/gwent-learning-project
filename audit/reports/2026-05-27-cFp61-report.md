## Behavior Changed

No gameplay behavior changed. cFp61 adds benchmark-only sampler materialization:

- Builds opponent-seat known preset source multisets from official starter main decks.
- Subtracts public/acting-visible opponent-preset cards from public board rows, row horns, discards, weather, removed-from-game public transform zones, acting visible hand, and prompt-revealed hand cards without iterating hidden opponent hand/deck identities.
- Counts prompt-revealed opponent hand cards once: they remain in the real opponent hand count while `fixedKnownHandSourceCounts` reduces only the unknown hand draw count.
- Samples hidden opponent source multisets in memory with deterministic public seeds.
- Validates sample counts and duplicate limits.
- Writes aggregate-only artifact rows with sample count stats, unique-count stats, duplicate-pressure buckets, and hand/deck overlap buckets.

No PIMC, ISMCTS, MCTS, rollouts, action evaluation, search move selection, product AI wiring, AI policy behavior, engine rules, legal moves, deck/catalog data, benchmark match records, failure-mining/rating artifacts, UI gameplay, or product difficulty changed.

## Deviations

The repair intentionally changes the final readout from all-valid to partial-valid with safe invalid roots. The stricter consistency check now rejects roots where the opponent-seat known preset prior remaining count is insufficient for the observed hidden hand/deck count after fixed prompt-revealed hand cards are accounted for. Current has 63 invalid roots and robust has 341 invalid roots, all with `insufficient_prior_remaining`.

## Risks And Follow-Ups

- cFp61 samples source multisets only; it does not clone determinized `MatchState`s, evaluate legal actions, or run rollouts.
- Decoy/control-shift and prompt-reveal states require conservation between the opponent preset prior, fixed known hand cards, public/acting-visible known cards, observed opponent deck count, and observed opponent hand count. The repaired sampler refuses inconsistent roots rather than shrinking the hand count or peeking at hidden opponent hand/deck identities.
- A future determinized probe must keep artifacts aggregate-only and must measure determinization risk before strength claims.
