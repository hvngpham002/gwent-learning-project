## Behavior Changed

- `cardStrategicValue(...)` now accepts `{ includeMedicAbilityBonus? }` option; defaults to `true` for backward compatibility with prompt-target ranking and discard-cost prompts
- `scorePlayMove(...)` passes `{ includeMedicAbilityBonus: false }` for Medic sources, replacing the flat `+260` with contextual `medicSourceUtilityScore`
- `estimateImmediateTempo(...)` injects `medicReviveTempoForSource` for own-row Medic source plays (non-Spy best printed strength)
- `SeatObservation` now includes `ownDiscard: SeatCardSummary[]` populated from the engine discard pile
- `AiDecisionTrace` includes `medicTimingAnalysis` for playing-phase decisions when at least one Medic play is legal
- `buildMedicTimingAnalysis` reuses `medicReviveCandidateValue`, `MEDIC_WEAK_TARGET_UTILITY`, `MEDIC_MEDIUM_TARGET_UTILITY` from policy to prevent threshold drift
- `medicPlayCandidateCount` counts unique Medic card instances (`sourceCardId`), not catalog identities
- `scanMedicTimingAnalysis` detects raw card names (one-word proper names and multi-word names), source IDs (hyphenated namespace.name patterns), and ability arrays in `medicTimingAnalysis` subtrees
- Test mock `baseObservation` now includes `ownDiscard: []` to prevent runtime crashes

## Hidden-Info Safety Notes

- `ownDiscard` contains only the acting seat's own discard pile (public game information)
- `medicTimingAnalysis` exposes only aggregate counts and buckets
- No card names, source IDs, instance IDs, raw card IDs, ability arrays, or linked source IDs appear in `medicTimingAnalysis`
- The `?? []` defensive guard in `buildMedicTimingAnalysis` prevents crashes when test mocks omit `ownDiscard`
- `scanMedicTimingAnalysis` enforces Medic-timing-specific forbidden tokens (raw card names, source IDs, ability arrays) via dedicated scanner
- Shared Medic helper constants (`medicReviveCandidateValue`, `MEDIC_WEAK_TARGET_UTILITY`, `MEDIC_MEDIUM_TARGET_UTILITY`) are exported from policy to prevent drift between trace diagnostics and policy scoring
