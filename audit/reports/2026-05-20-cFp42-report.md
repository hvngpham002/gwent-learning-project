## Risks And Follow-Ups

- The casebook is based on starter-deck v1-vs-v0 artifacts. Competitive and
  mechanics decks may produce different resource-pressure patterns.
- The casebook does not prove a policy patch. cFp43 should first convert
  representative overinvestment rows into deterministic fixtures, then tune
  against those fixtures.
- `round_three_low_resource` remains useful as a downstream validation metric,
  but it should not drive a direct round-3 pass patch by itself.
