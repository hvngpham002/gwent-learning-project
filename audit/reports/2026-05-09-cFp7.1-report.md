## Annotation Finalization

Completed the final sections in:
`docs/research/literature/ai/annotations/huang2022invalidmasking/huang2022invalidmasking.md`.

The completed decision records:

- `huang2022invalidmasking` is a direct implementation source for future
  policy-gradient / neural-policy legal-action masking.
- Future Gwent ML wrappers should derive masks from engine legal moves
  and use those masks for both sampling and gradient log-probability
  calculation.
- Invalid-action penalties should not be the primary legality mechanism
  for a large legal/illegal action space.
- Naive masking, where sampling uses a masked distribution but gradient
  updates use an unmasked distribution, should be avoided.
- The paper does not justify hidden-information shortcuts, a specific
  Gwent action encoding, game-strength evaluation, exploitability, or
  immediate AI implementation by itself.
