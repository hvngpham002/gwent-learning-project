## Behavior Changed

No gameplay behavior changed. cFp67 adds benchmark-only analysis/evaluation
infrastructure:

- reads committed post-cFp66 cFp61 materialization, cFp62 invalid-root, and
  cFp64 provenance artifacts;
- joins invalid roots to provenance records by public scalar root metadata;
- emits one scalar-only cFp67 record for every remaining invalid sampler root;

## Risks And Follow-Ups

- cFp68 must not silently drop invalid sampler roots. It should report skipped
  root counts and percentages beside every probe result.
- The cFp67 recommendation is valid-root-only probing, not evidence that the
  remaining invalid roots are solved.
- A later deeper public-state reconstruction phase may still be useful, but
  cFp67 does not identify a narrow repair class likely to cover a meaningful
  portion of the remaining roots.
