## Annotation Finalization

The annotation now records:

- `pages_read: "1-9"`
- `status: "complete"`

The final sections now decide:

- `lanctot2009monte` is a CFR-family training-method source for later
  reduced MCCFR experiments, not a full-engine implementation mandate.
- External-sampling MCCFR is the strongest offline reduced-experiment
  candidate from the paper because it preserves high-probability regret
  bounds while reducing traversal cost in balanced games.
- Outcome-sampling MCCFR remains a later online/adaptive-opponent
  candidate because it can use opponent play samples but depends on
  exploration and positive sampling probabilities.
- Any Gwent use must preserve information-set hidden-info boundaries,
  avoid claiming reduced-game exploitability as full-engine robustness,
  compare sampling variants empirically, and keep legal-action
  tensorization grounded in the action-masking literature rather than
  this paper alone.
