# Cluster C Phase 18 Report — Remaining Leader Ability Rules Matrix

This phase is documentation/audit only. It implements
`docs/spec/2026-05-02-cCp18-specs.md` by producing a durable leader rules
matrix at `docs/leader-ability-matrix.md`, updating
`docs/PROJECT_STATE.md`, and writing this report.

No production TypeScript, tests, catalog data, deck presets, images/assets,
UI routes/CSS/components, engine legal moves or command execution, AI
policy, simulation/export code, or package/config/CI scripts were changed.

## 1. Executive Summary

cEp8 closed the authentic UI gap for multi-move leaders by adding a
generic leader-choice menu. The remaining work to make the 22 official
leaders fully playable is a rule-clarity problem, not a UI problem. cCp18
audits every placeholder leader, identifies local-source conflicts, and
recommends ranked implementation tranches that cCp19+ specs can pull from
without re-running the audit.

Key outputs:

- 22 catalog leader source records enumerated; 7 active executable, 1
  passive implemented, 14 placeholder.
- 14 placeholder leaders classified into 10 shared implementation patterns
  (row-wide horn-like passives, ongoing spy snapshot modifier, deck
  tutor / discard cost, discard restore, opponent hand information
  disclosure, leader cancel, setup-time draw, random Special replay,
  optimize agile rows, both-discard recycle).
- 10 local-source conflicts recorded and classified (`docs/leader-ability-
  matrix.md` § Source Conflicts §C-1 through §C-10). Three are blockers
  for the affected tranche: §C-2 (`Emhyr: The Relentless` catalog vs
  rulebook), §C-1 (`Eredin: Destroyer of Worlds` rulebook §16 mis-
  attribution), and §C-4 (`optimize_agile_rows` choice vs auto wording).
- Four cCp19+ implementation tranches ranked by ambiguity, blast radius,
  starter-deck reach, UI / prompt complexity, hidden-info risk, and
  testability:
  - **Tranche 1** — Row-Wide Horn-Like Passives (`double_siege`,
    `double_close` ×2, `double_ranged`). Lowest ambiguity, no UI churn,
    no hidden-info risk.
  - **Tranche 2** — Active Score Modifier and Single-Step Prompt Leaders
    (`double_spies`, `restore_discard_to_hand`, `optimize_agile_rows`,
    `shuffle_discards_into_decks`, `random_medic`, contingent
    `draw_opponent_discard`).
  - **Tranche 3** — Setup-Time Event and Multi-Step Prompt
    (`draw_extra_card`, `discard_two_draw_one_from_deck`).
  - **Tranche 4** — Hidden-Info Disclosure and Suppression
    (`look_three_cards`, `cancel_leader`).
- 10 open product questions recorded as blockers for cCp19+ (matrix
  § Open Product Questions).

### Pattern groupings

10 implementation patterns are documented in
`docs/leader-ability-matrix.md` § Shared Implementation Patterns:

1. Row-wide horn-like passives (`double_siege`, `double_close` ×2,
   `double_ranged`).
2. Ongoing spy score modifier — snapshot at fire time (`double_spies`).
3. Deck tutor / discard cost — multi-step prompt
   (`discard_two_draw_one_from_deck`).
4. Discard restore — single-step prompt (`restore_discard_to_hand`,
   contingent `draw_opponent_discard`).
5. Opponent hand information disclosure (`look_three_cards`).
6. Leader cancel / suppression (`cancel_leader`).
7. Setup-time hand-size modifier (`draw_extra_card`).
8. Random Special replay from discard (`random_medic`).
9. Bulk move friendly Agile units (`optimize_agile_rows`).
10. Both-discard recycle (`shuffle_discards_into_decks`).

The spec's expected groupings (§ "Group By Implementation Pattern"
1-10) are largely preserved with two refinements:

- The spec's group 4 ("Spy / board-state modifier") is renamed
  "Ongoing spy score modifier (snapshot)" because the catalog text
  "spy cards already on the battlefield" implies snapshot semantics
  rather than ongoing.
- The spec's group 3 ("Discard restore / steal") is split into the
  *acting-seat discard* (`restore_discard_to_hand`) and *opponent
  discard* (`draw_opponent_discard`, contingent on §C-2) because the
  hidden-info implications differ if §C-2 resolves to the rulebook
  reading.

## 8. Recommended Implementation Tranches

Full ranking and rationale in `docs/leader-ability-matrix.md`
§ Recommended Implementation Tranches.

### Tranche 1 — Row-Wide Horn-Like Passives

- Members: `double_siege`, `double_close` (×2), `double_ranged`.
- Engine surface: new `getRowHornPolicyBySeat(state, leaders)` helper
  mirroring cCp15's `getWeatherPolicyBySeat`. `calculateScores` reads
  the policy and applies ×2 to non-hero unit strength on the friendly
  mapped row, suppressed by an existing Commander's Horn on that row.
- Legal moves: none (passive, no `use_leader` move).
- Hidden-info risk: none.
- Why first: smallest engine surface, zero hidden-info risk, no UI
  churn, four leader records across three factions, excellent
  testability.

### Tranche 2 — Active Score Modifier and Single-Step Prompt Leaders

- Members: `double_spies`, `restore_discard_to_hand`,
  `optimize_agile_rows`, `shuffle_discards_into_decks`, `random_medic`,
  contingent `draw_opponent_discard`.
- Engine surfaces: existing `pendingPrompt` machinery plus snapshot
  fields and modifier markers. No new prompt kinds required for these
  five-six.
- Suggested internal ordering by simplicity:
  1. `double_spies` (snapshot).
  2. `optimize_agile_rows` (instantaneous board mutation, possible
     row-choice menu reusing cEp8).
  3. `restore_discard_to_hand` (single-step `choose_card` prompt).
  4. `random_medic` (random pick, plays through normal Special path).
  5. `shuffle_discards_into_decks` (bulk move, deterministic shuffle).
  6. `draw_opponent_discard` (only if §C-2 resolves "catalog wins").

### Tranche 3 — Setup-Time Event and Multi-Step Prompt

- Members: `draw_extra_card`, `discard_two_draw_one_from_deck`.
- `draw_extra_card` is trivial in `setup.ts` but exercises the
  setup-time-leader contract (no `use_leader`, no `leaderUsed`,
  same shape as King Bran's passive).
- `discard_two_draw_one_from_deck` introduces the engine's first
  multi-stage prompt; doing it before Tranche 4 keeps the hidden-info
  tranche free of prompt-stage churn.

### Tranche 4 — Hidden-Info Disclosure and Suppression

- Members: `look_three_cards`, `cancel_leader`. Plus
  `draw_opponent_discard` if §C-2 resolves to "rulebook wins"
  (own-deck tutor with hidden-info ramifications).
- `look_three_cards` is the engine's first persistent hidden-info
  disclosure. Test coverage must verify the opponent perspective
  stays redacted (`legal-heuristic-v0`, simulation export, AI safe
  observers).
- `cancel_leader` cross-cuts every passive implemented in
  Tranches 1-3 and must come last so test fixtures can demonstrate
  suppression.

- **R-1 (high) — §C-2 product decision blocks Tranche 2 (or 4)
  ordering.** Until the product owner picks "catalog wins" vs
  "rulebook wins" for `Emhyr: The Relentless`, the
  `draw_opponent_discard` leader cannot be implemented and Tranche 2

  `docs/gwent-rules.md` §16.** Until cCp19 lands the §16 row edit,
  any reader who consults §16 first (rather than the matrix) will

  surface for `optimize_agile_rows`.** "Row of your choice" needs
  the cEp8 menu; "auto-optimal" does not. The chosen path

  `placeholderLeaderAbilityIds`.** A future contributor might
  assume the array is exhaustive. Recommendation: the next time
  `src/data/catalog/leaders/official-promotion.ts` is touched

  Tranche 2 implementation.** Renaming the catalog ability ID is
  a larger ripple than a description fix; cCp18 recommends
  preserving the ID and rewriting only the description.

  restore effects in `tests/catalog/linkedAbilityInvariants.test.ts`
  remains.** cCp17 left one `it.todo` scoped to placeholder leader

  promote it because no leader resolver lands in this phase. cCp19
  Tranche 2 should promote it as `restore_discard_to_hand` and
  `random_medic` come online.
- **RF-2 (follow-up) — King Bran integration tests for new
  passives.** Tranche 1's `double_*` row-horn passives must compose
  with King Bran's `weather_half_penalty` (already implemented).
  cCp19 specs should explicitly require coverage where King Bran is
  the seat's leader and the row-horn is contributed by, e.g., a
