## Key Findings

The strongest consensus is that Cluster F should not jump straight into
neural self-play. The immediate foundation should be annotation and
evaluation-ladder design.

Priority annotation order:

1. Cowling, Powley, Whitehouse - Information Set Monte Carlo Tree
   Search.
2. Cowling, Ward, Powley - Ensemble Determinization in Magic: The
   Gathering.
3. Long, Sturtevant, Buro, Furtak - Perfect Information Monte Carlo
   Sampling analysis.
4. Zinkevich et al. - CFR.
5. Lanctot et al. - MCCFR.
6. Huang and Ontanon - invalid action masking.
7. Timbers et al. - approximate exploitability.
8. Hearthstone / LOCM benchmark papers.

Most likely implementation direction after annotation: evaluation
ladder first, then an ISMCTS / determinization baseline or
literature-justified `legal-heuristic-v1`. CFR-family methods should
ground self-play/exploitability claims and may support reduced-game
experiments later.
