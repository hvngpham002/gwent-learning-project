## Policy Interface And Observation Behavior

`EnginePolicy` exposes `id` and `selectMove(input)`, where input contains the acting seat, a `SeatObservation`, and the exact legal move list for that seat. The policy returns one of those legal move objects or `null`.

The default observation includes own hand card summaries, own leader/pass/gem/deck/discard counts, opponent hand count only, opponent leader public status, opponent deck/discard/pass/gem counts, public board/weather summaries, score breakdowns from `calculateScores`, and acting-seat prompt summaries.

The observation does not include opponent hand card IDs, source IDs, names, rows, abilities, or deck order.

## Policy Decision Rules

`legal-heuristic-v0` is deterministic and uses no randomness, timers, date input, or legacy AI path.

Decision order:

1. Return `null` if no legal moves exist.
2. Resolve prompts first, preferring the highest visible target strength and then `moveId`.
3. During mulligan, choose the legal zero-card mulligan move.
4. If the opponent has passed and AI is ahead, choose Pass.
5. Choose the best legal card play by own-hand printed strength, kind, target, and `moveId`.
6. Use implemented Clear Weather leader only when public weather exists.
7. Fall back to Pass when available.

## Shell Integration Behavior

`EngineGameManager` now calls `getLegalHeuristicAiCommand`. The controller:

- does nothing when no match exists;
- does nothing during adapter locks except an AI-owned prompt lock;
- does nothing after game end;
- waits for human mulligan before AI mulligan;
- acts only on AI playing turns;
- blocks human-owned prompts;
- allows AI-owned prompts to resolve through legal `choose_prompt_option` moves;
- does not auto-resolve `round_end`.

All AI actions still go through `dispatchEngineCommand`.

## Hidden-Info Discipline

The default human engine view still renders AI hand count only. The policy observation may include AI own-hand details when the AI is the acting seat, but it only includes opponent hand count. No debug observation or UI display exposing hidden opponent hand details was added.

## Deviations / Limits

- `round_end` remains explicit and is not auto-resolved by AI or UI.
- `legal-heuristic-v0` is intentionally weak; it ranks basic plays by visible own-hand printed strength and simple public context.
- Browser click-through was not run; route smoke and Redux/controller tests cover the required cDp5 behavior.
- No AI-vs-AI simulation, ML export, deck builder, Card Studio, PvP, default-route engine gameplay, or placeholder leader implementation was added.
