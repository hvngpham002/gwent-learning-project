# Cluster C Phase 7 Report - Scoring Pipeline and Scorch Resolution

## Summary

Added a pure core scoring pipeline for normalized match state and used it to resolve Special Scorch and Unit Scorch Close in the pure command/ability path. The pipeline returns deterministic, serializable, explainable score breakdowns with row totals, card traces, active weather, and diagnostics.

## Files Changed

- `src/game/core/scoring.ts`: new pure scoring pipeline, weather detection, modifier ordering, King Bran weather policy hook, Scorch target helpers, and diagnostics.
- `src/game/core/index.ts`: exports scoring APIs.
- `src/game/core/types.ts`: adds Scorch movement reasons and `scorch_resolved` events.
- `src/game/core/commands.ts`: resolves Special Scorch with effective strength and discards the Scorch card after effect.
- `src/game/core/abilities.ts`: resolves Unit Scorch Close after placement using scoring.
- `tests/game/coreScoring.test.ts`: adds scoring pipeline coverage.
- `tests/game/coreCommands.test.ts`: updates Special Scorch and Unit Scorch command behavior tests.
- `tests/game/coreAbilities.test.ts`: updates Unit Scorch Close deferral expectation now that scoring exists.

## Behavior Added

- `calculateScores({ state, catalogCards, catalogLeaders, weatherPolicyBySeat })` calculates:
  - total per seat;
  - row totals per seat;
  - card-level printed, weathered, Tight Bond, Morale, Horn, and final strength values;
  - active weather effects from `state.weather.entries`;
  - diagnostics for missing card/source and impossible multi-Horn states.
- Weather supports Frost, Fog, Rain, and Skellige Storm. Duplicate weather effects do not stack.
- Heroes keep printed strength and ignore external scoring modifiers.
- Special row placeholders such as Decoy score 0 and are not Unit/Scorch candidates.
- Modifier order is exactly Weather, Tight Bond, Morale Boost, Commander's Horn.
- Tight Bond counts same-source allied Tight Bond units on the same side of the battlefield.
- Morale Boost is row-local and excludes each Morale source from its own bonus.
- Special Horn and Unit Horn apply once per row with defensive diagnostics for impossible multiple Horn sources.
- `weatherPolicyBySeat` supports `"king_bran"` mitigation for friendly weathered non-Hero Units.
- `findSpecialScorchTargets` and `findUnitScorchCloseTargets` use effective strengths and exclude Heroes/placeholders.

## Scorch Resolution

- Special Scorch now executes as a legal one-shot special:
  - targets tied highest effective-strength non-Hero Units across both sides;
  - can destroy the acting seat's own units;
  - moves destroyed cards to their current controller discard piles;
  - moves the Scorch card itself to the acting seat discard pile;
  - emits `ability_triggered`, `card_moved`, `scorch_resolved`, and `ability_resolved`.
- Unit Scorch Close now resolves after the unit is placed:
  - checks the opposite close row total against the 10-strength threshold;
  - destroys tied highest effective-strength non-Hero Units on that row;
  - emits `below_threshold`, `no_targets`, or `destroyed` outcomes.
- Scorched cards with planned discard triggers (`summon`, `avenger`) emit `ability_deferred` with `discard_trigger_pending`; replacement remains out of scope.
