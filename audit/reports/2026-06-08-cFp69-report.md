No gameplay behavior changed. cFp69 adds benchmark-only probe scaffold
infrastructure:

- reads committed cFp68 eligible and skipped contract artifacts;
- re-observes matching benchmark roots through the existing headless root
  observer;
- filters probe work to cFp68 eligible roots only;
- emits one hidden-info-safe `probe-roots.jsonl` record per eligible root;
