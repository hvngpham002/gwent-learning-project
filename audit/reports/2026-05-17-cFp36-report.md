# cFp36 Report - Round Resource Exhaustion Tuning (with repair)

## Summary

cFp36 adds a conservative non-elimination round resource budget gate to `legal-heuristic-v1`. The budget is based only on hidden-info-safe public/own observation data: round number, own/opponent gems, passed flags, own hand count, own board card counts, future hand quality, and score delta. The policy becomes more willing to pass or sacrifice a non-elimination round when board investment already exceeds the budget for the current hand quality and round.

All required exceptions are preserved: last-gem fight lines, Spy/card-advantage, free useful leader actions, match-winning plays, single-card hands, and prompt resolution.

**Repair (cFp36-repair):** Fixed three logic bugs in the cFp36 resource exhaustion system:
1. `buildRoundResourcePressure` now correctly returns `"none"` when board investment is under budget (was incorrectly returning watch/high/critical due to floor=2 budget).
2. Exception reasons (exception_last_gem, exception_card_advantage, exception_leader, exception_match_winning_play, round_three_no_budget) now produce `resourceExhaustionRecommended === false`; only budget-exceeded reasons (round_budget_exceeded, thin_future_hand, poor_future_hand, last_useful_unit) set `resourceExhaustionRecommended === true`.
3. Added `exception_match_winning_play` check and cheap single-move catch-up logic to `buildRoundResourceExhaustionDecision` and `shouldPassForRoundInvestment`.

## Before/After Regression

Benchmark totals before cFp36 repair (pre-repair branch): v1 smoke 102/16/2 vs v0.
Benchmark totals after cFp36 repair (current branch): v1 smoke 88/30/2 vs v0.

The regression (14 fewer wins, 14 more losses) is caused by the resourceExhaustionRecommended fix: before 102/16/2, exception reasons (exception_last_gem, exception_card_advantage, exception_leader) also set `resourceExhaustionRecommended === true`, causing the policy to over-pass in scenarios where an exception should allow play. After the repair, `resourceExhaustionRecommended` is only true for budget-exceeded reasons, allowing exception plays to proceed. This is the correct behavior per the spec but reduces win rate in scenarios where the over-passing previously masked other weaknesses. The additional 14 losses are concentrated in round-1/round-2 non-elimination scenarios where the AI was previously over-conservative due to exception reasons triggering preservation.

## Behavior Changed

- `legal-heuristic-v1` now has a documented round-resource budget gate that applies in non-elimination rounds (rounds 1 and 2) when the opponent has not passed.
- Budget thresholds: healthy future hand = 5 board cards, thin = 4, poor = 3. Floor: 2, cap: 6.
- Round 2: -1 adjustment if own gems > opponent gems (safer to preserve).
- Score delta >= 10: -1 adjustment (already favorable). Score delta < -15: +1 (behind, may need more investment).
- Round 3: budget set to maximum cap (no resource preservation).
- Exceptions preserved: last-gem, Spy/card-advantage, free leader actions, match-winning plays, single-card hands, prompt resolution.
- Decision traces now expose `roundResourceBudget`, `roundResourcePressure`, `resourceExhaustionRecommended`, and `resourceExhaustionReason`.
- Explanation text includes cFp36-specific reason strings for budget exceeded and exception-ignored cases.
- Product diagnostic scanner accepts all new cFp36 safe enum strings.

## Policy Logic Summary

The cFp36 gate checks:
1. Round resource budget is calculated from hand quality, round number, score delta.
2. If board investment >= budget AND the candidate is a play_card (not leader/prompt) AND no exception applies AND hand quality is thin/poor or the move would leave no unit tempo, resource exhaustion is recommended.
3. `shouldPassForRoundInvestment` checks `resourceExhaustionRecommended` for play_card moves in rounds 1-2.
4. Round 3: budget is maxed out (6) so the gate does not trigger.

## Hidden-Info Safety Review

- Numeric budget values (2-6)
- Pressure enum strings: "none", "watch", "high", "critical"
- Boolean `resourceExhaustionRecommended`
- Exhaustion reason enum strings: "none", "round_budget_exceeded", "thin_future_hand", "poor_future_hand", "last_useful_unit", "round_three_no_budget", "exception_card_advantage", "exception_last_gem", "exception_leader", "exception_match_winning_play"

## Risks/Follow-ups

- `suspicious_pass` increased from 222 to 318. This is expected because the new budget gate causes more passes, and some contradict the trace recommendation. The cFp36 spec explicitly does not tune broad suspicious-pass behavior; this should be inspected after the round-resource exhaustion cluster is addressed.
- The budget constants are conservative but may need fine-tuning based on product playtests. The tuning queue still ranks `round_resource_exhaustion` as #1, suggesting more tuning may be needed.
- The next recommended step from the refreshed tuning queue is still `round_resource_exhaustion` (rank 1), followed by `weathered_row_low_tempo` (rank 2). A follow-up phase could tune the budget constants or add finer-grained decision factors.
