## Behavior Changed

No gameplay behavior changed. cFp62 adds benchmark-only invalid-root analysis:

- Reruns the current cFp61 materialization path through the benchmark root observer.
- Emits one hidden-info-safe scalar/count record per invalid root only.
- Classifies every invalid root exactly once with deterministic first-match order.
- Serializes deterministic `manifest.json`, `summary.json`, `invalid-roots.jsonl`, and `report.md` artifacts under `sampler-invalid-roots/cFp62/`.

move selection, product AI wiring, AI policy behavior, engine rules, legal
moves, benchmark suite definitions, benchmark match records, failure-mining
classifiers/artifacts, rating artifacts, UI gameplay, catalog/deck data, product
difficulty, cFp61 artifact rewrite, or browser benchmark execution changed.
