## Behavior Changed

- Name uniqueness is auto-suffix based. Names are trimmed/whitespace-normalized and conflicts become `Name 2`, `Name 3`, etc.
- Existing browser-local duplicate decks are normalized on both read and write through the existing storage normalization path.
- New decks show a visible faction selector immediately. Existing decks can change faction through the same catalog-derived selector.
- Faction change removes wrong-faction cards after confirmation, resets the leader to the new faction default, keeps neutral cards, and clears side decks.
- The card pool hides wrong-faction cards through faction-aware filtering. Relevant cards that cannot be added remain visible through the card image and overlay badge, with reasons such as `3/3 limit` or `special cap reached` kept in metadata/title text rather than extra labels under the card.
- Special-cap disabled states are visually dimmed and programmatically enforced through the pure add-state helper and disabled buttons/card clicks.
- Duplicate creates a selected same-composition local copy with unique ID/name.
- Reset to catalog is available for catalog-derived local decks, requires confirmation, restores catalog composition/faction/leader/name, and preserves the local `presetId`.
- Import still never overwrites. If conflict handling changes the imported ID or final name, the builder shows an import notice naming the final imported deck.
