## Risks / Follow-ups

- The robust suite is still a fixed official-starter matrix. It increases sample
  volume but does not replace holdout suites, mechanics/competitive deck pools,
  or approximate best-response probes.
- Failure-mining findings are triage signals. The broad `suspicious_pass` count
  should not drive tuning without a follow-up casebook.
- Ratings are suite-local. Do not compare robust ratings against current or
  expanded suite ratings as if they share one rating universe.
