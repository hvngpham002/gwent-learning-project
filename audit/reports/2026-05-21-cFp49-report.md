Cluster decision:

  `round_one_overinvestment` is durable and loss-correlated, but public rows do

  mostly wins with no visible positive unit tempo.
- `weathered_row_low_tempo`: analyzer noise or broad signal.
- `medic_no_target_timing`: watch-only.
- `skellige_matchup_skew`: watch-only.
- `remaining_suspicious_pass`: analyzer noise or broad signal.

Recommended cFp50 scope: hidden-info-safe failure-mining guard-state telemetry
centered on remaining round-one overinvestment before another behavior patch.
