## Behavior Changed

`legal-heuristic-v1` now finalizes every selected playing-phase `play_card` through the existing cFp43 round-one overinvestment analysis for the exact selected candidate. If pass is legal, round is 1, the AI has more than one gem, the opponent has not passed, and the selected hand-spending play reports `roundOneOverinvestmentRecommended === true` for `round_one_board_limit` or `round_one_low_future_hand`, the policy selects `pass`.

Existing exceptions remain unblocked: Spy/card-advantage, match-winning play, cheap single-move catch-up, `use_leader`, opponent-passed, last-gem, single-card-hand, and `exception_*` cases.

No thresholds, cumulative budgeting, engine rules, legal moves, prompts, scoring, UI behavior, catalog data, deck presets, ratings formulas, search/training code, or product difficulty changed.
