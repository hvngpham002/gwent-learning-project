## Behavior Changed

Test-only browser coverage changed. No production behavior changed.

The implementation adds test-local Playwright helpers for local deck seeding, authentic mulligan/start flow, and pass/resolve match-end progression. No production testability helper, engine shortcut, Redux state injection, fake `RoundResult`, timer-based rule advancement, UI layout/styling change, or engine rule/legal-move change was added.

### Weather-Card Target Smoke

- Adds `authentic match targets the Weather panel for a selected hand weather card (cEp11.1)`.
- Seeds a browser-local Northern Realms deck via `gwent_authentic_decks_v1`. The fixture has 22 battlefield cards and 10 legal weather specials.
- Uses a bounded deterministic seed list (`cep111-weather-1` through `cep111-weather-4`) until a public weather `data-source-id` is visible in the human hand.
- Drives normal product flow: pre-game deck selection, begin match, keep mulligan, acknowledge `Start the match?`, wait for human action, select a visible weather card, and click `authentic-weather-target`.
- Asserts `authentic-target-groups[data-target-state="has-targets"]`, hint copy `target the weather panel`, target aria `Play <weather card> on the weather panel`, no right-rail fallback action, selected public weather source count increasing in the Weather panel, `Human played` activity, stale target cleanup, and hidden-info-safe visible text.

### Match-End Ledger Smoke

- Adds `authentic match-end ledger is reachable through product controls (cEp11.1)`.
- Starts a direct authentic match, keeps mulligan, acknowledges `Start the match?`, then reaches game end through visible product controls only.
- The flow is intentionally pass-only: the human clicks `pass`; `legal-heuristic-v0` advances AI actions from legal moves; the human clicks `resolve round`; non-game-end ledgers are dismissed with `next round`. A hard four-round limit guards against brittle loops.
- Asserts the `data-ledger-kind="match_end"` overlay, `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, match-concluded title/eyebrow, at least two real round-history rows, real standing rows, no placeholder progression labels (`mmr`, `rank`, `streak`, `xp`, `reward`, `elo`), Escape persistence, hidden-info-safe visible text, and rematch routing through a fresh mulligan/start-match flow with the old overlay cleared.

## Deviations

- The match-end smoke covers rematch, not the setup/change-deck branch. The rematch branch is the cleanest deterministic route in this phase; the setup branch remains a documented browser-smoke follow-up.
- The match-end product path uses direct match entry plus visible pass/resolve controls instead of a local opponent fixture. This avoids adding product behavior or opponent-selection test hooks while still reaching real engine game end.

## Risks / Follow-Ups

- Add a separate setup/change-deck branch smoke if that navigation path becomes a regression risk.
- The match-end smoke is intentionally a pass-only tripwire. It does not replace broader strategic full-match automation.
- Future drag/drop or card-flight phases should keep the cEp11.1 weather-target and match-end assertions passing while adding their own interaction coverage.
