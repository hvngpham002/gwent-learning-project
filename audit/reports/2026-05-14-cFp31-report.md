# cFp31 Report - Legal-Heuristic-v1 Round Investment And Future-Hand Preservation

## Overview

Implements round-investment preservation for `legal-heuristic-v1` to prevent the AI from overcommitting Round 1 and exhausting its hand, leaving Rounds 2/3 as low-card formalities.

## Files Changed

- `src/game/ai/decisionTrace.ts` — Added `AiDecisionRoundInvestmentRisk`, `AiDecisionRoundInvestmentRecommendation`, `AiDecisionRoundInvestmentAnalysis` types and `roundInvestmentAnalysis` field to `AiDecisionTrace`.
- `src/game/ai/legalHeuristicPolicyV1.ts` — Added `buildLegalHeuristicV1RoundInvestmentAnalysis`, `shouldPassForRoundInvestment`, integrated round-investment gate into `choosePlayingMove`.
- `src/game/ai/explainLegalHeuristicV1Decision.ts` — Added `roundInvestmentAnalysis` building and `policy-round-investment` reason kind handling.
- `src/game/ai/productPolicies.ts` — Updated `legal-heuristic-v1` metadata: `latestPhase: "cFp31"`, spec/report paths, `"round-investment preservation"` capability.
- `src/components/gwent/matchDiagnostics.ts` — Added `scanRoundInvestmentAnalysis` scanner.
- `tests/components/gwent/authenticAiLabViewModel.test.ts` — Updated cFp31 phase metadata expectations and capability assertions.

## Behavior Changed

`legal-heuristic-v1` now evaluates round-investment risk before selecting a playing move. The new gate:

1. **Preserves future hand** when ahead or near-even (`scoreDelta >= -10`) and the best useful move would burn the last useful future cards (no positive unit/hero moves remain, or hand quality is `poor`/`thin` with estimated hand count ≤ 2 after the move).
2. **Sacrifices non-elimination rounds** when behind (`scoreDelta < 0`), no single-move catch-up exists, and future-hand risk is `high`.
3. **Critical risk** triggers when estimated hand after the move ≤ 1 and no positive unit moves remain, unless the move wins the match.
4. **Exceptions**: Last-gem rounds (`ownGems <= 1`) always fight. Single-card hands (`ownHandCount <= 1`) always play. Card-advantage moves (Spy) are never blocked.

## Deviations

1. **Benchmark artifacts not refreshed**: The `benchmarkArtifacts.test.ts` spawn EINVAL issue (pre-existing on Windows) prevents `npx tsx`-based benchmark execution. Totals carry forward from cFp30.
2. **Threshold calibration**: The round-investment thresholds were tightened during testing. `shouldPassForRoundInvestment` requires `ownHandCount > 2` for critical-risk blocking and `estimatedHandCountAfterSelectedMove >= 2` for preservation checks, to avoid over-passing on small hands (e.g., 2-card hands with frost/weather).

## Hidden-Info Safety

- `roundInvestmentAnalysis` uses only public `SeatObservation` data (board counts, hand counts, gem counts, round number, legal-move scoring).
- No card identities, source IDs, instance IDs, ability arrays, or hidden hand details are exposed.
- `scanRoundInvestmentAnalysis` validates safe enum strings and rejects raw identifiers.
- `estimatedHandCountAfterSelectedMove` is an approximation (does not reveal drawn card identities).

## Risks/Follow-ups

1. **Windows benchmark execution**: `spawn EINVAL` blocks artifact refresh; needs separate investigation.
2. **Threshold tuning**: Current thresholds are conservative but may need adjustment based on playtest data.
3. **Spy-only hands**: The card-advantage check uses ability detection; edge cases with custom Spy-like abilities should be monitored.
