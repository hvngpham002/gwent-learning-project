## Behavior Changed

No gameplay behavior changed. cFp65 adds benchmark-only public-zone accounting
diagnostics to the existing sampler artifact paths:

- public opponent-owned cards are classified as main-deck-attributable,
  known side-deck-only, or off-prior using public zones and known preset prior
  metadata only;
- only main-deck-attributable public cards consume the known main-deck prior;
- side-deck-only and off-prior public cards are serialized only as scalar public
  adjustment counts;
- strict conservation remains enforced through uncovered prior deficit counts;
- invalid roots remain invalid unless public-only accounting covers the deficit.

No hidden opponent hand/deck card identity is used to make an invalid root valid.
The implementation reads opponent hidden hand/deck lengths only, never hidden
hand/deck source ids, instance ids, catalog ids, abilities, or card names.

No PIMC, ISMCTS, MCTS, rollouts, action evaluation, action ranking, search move
selection, product AI behavior, engine rules, legal moves, benchmark suite
definitions, benchmark match records, rating formulas/artifacts, UI gameplay,
catalog/deck data, product difficulty, or browser benchmark execution changed.
