# cFp33 Report - Legal-Heuristic-v1 Scoia'tael First-Turn Choice Strategy

## Summary

cFp33 replaces `legal-heuristic-v1`'s deterministic `scoiatael-first-player:self`
prompt fallback with a deterministic, hidden-info-safe post-mulligan hand-shape
heuristic. The AI now chooses `go first` for proactive hands (Spy, Muster,
strong tempo) and `let opponent start` for reactive hands (weather/scorch-heavy
with weak proactive tempo).

## Behavior Changed

- `legal-heuristic-v1` no longer always chooses `scoiatael-first-player:self` for
  Scoia'tael first-player prompts.
- v1 now computes initiative vs reaction scores from hand shape and selects
  `self` or `opponent` based on the threshold `reactionScore >= initiativeScore + 12`.
- `AiDecisionTrace` now includes `scoiataelFirstTurnAnalysis` (nullable) for
  Scoia'tael prompt decisions, exposing counts, scores, tempo bucket, and reason
  enum kinds.
- `explainLegalHeuristicV1Decision` now returns matching analysis and human-readable
  reason strings for Scoia'tael decisions.
- Product diagnostics scan now includes `scanScoiataelFirstTurnAnalysis` which
  rejects source IDs, card names, instance IDs, and ability arrays.
- `legal-heuristic-v0` is unchanged (still deterministic `self`).
- Engine rules, legal moves, catalog data, deck presets, and UI behavior are unchanged.

## Risks/Follow-ups

- The scoring constants (initiative/reaction bonuses) are heuristic and may need
  tuning after real-playtest data. The threshold of 12 points provides a safety
  margin against over-reactive behavior.

  productPolicies.ts and the policy doc.
