# cFp35 Report - Failure-Mining Calibration And Tuning Queue

## Summary

cFp35 calibrates the cFp34 failure-mining evaluator without changing AI policy
behavior. `suspicious_pass` no longer fires solely because
`roundInvestmentAnalysis.stopLossRecommended === false`; broad pass candidates
that are explained by preserve-future-hand, stop-loss, voluntary-safe, or
insufficient-context diagnostics are counted only as aggregate analyzer noise.

The failure-mining artifact bundle now includes deterministic
`tuning-queue.md`, and the recommended cFp36 scope is round-resource exhaustion
before broad suspicious-pass tuning.

## Behavior Changed

- Failure-mining evaluator behavior changed only for artifact generation.
- `suspicious_pass` now requires a high-signal contradiction, such as
  `recommendation === "continue"`, ignored single-move catch-up outside
  preserve/sacrifice cases, fight-last-gem pass when the policy upper bound can
  win, or active voluntary-unsafe pass with safe opponent-passed context.
- Suppressed suspicious-pass candidates are recorded only as aggregate counts.
- No `legal-heuristic-v1` scoring or move selection changed.
- No v0 behavior, engine rules, legal moves, UI behavior, catalog data, deck
  presets, H100/Slurm workflow, ratings, search, or training changed.

## Hidden-Info Safety Review

The hidden-info scanner now covers all five failure-mining artifact files. The
generated text does not include forbidden raw-debug or private-zone tokens:
`cardsById`, `finalState`, `commandLog`, `unsafeDebugResults`, `ownHand`,
`opponentHand`, raw `"hand":`, raw `"deck":`, `seat_a:`, or `seat_b:`.

Suppressed pass instrumentation stores only aggregate category counts. The
tuning queue stores only finding kinds, counts, public policy/deck/faction and
matchup ids, severity, and recommendations.
