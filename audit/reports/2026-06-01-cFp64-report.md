## Behavior Changed

No gameplay behavior changed. cFp64 adds benchmark-only provenance
infrastructure:

- Reruns the current cFp61/cFp62 root-observer path.
- Emits one scalar/count-only provenance record for each cFp62
  `public_zone_count_deficit` invalid root.
- Classifies each provenance root exactly once with deterministic precedence:
  round-end, zero-zone guard, single-zone labels, mixed zones, fallback.
- Writes deterministic `manifest.json`, `summary.json`,
  `provenance-roots.jsonl`, and `report.md` artifacts under
  `sampler-public-zone-provenance/cFp64/`.

No PIMC, ISMCTS, MCTS, rollout search, action evaluation, action ranking,
search move selection, product AI wiring, AI policy behavior, engine rules,
legal moves, benchmark suite definitions, benchmark match records,
failure-mining classifiers/artifacts, rating artifacts, UI gameplay,
catalog/deck data, product difficulty, cFp61/cFp62 artifact rewrite, or browser
benchmark execution changed.
