## Behavior Changed

- **No-target Medic delay penalty:** When a Medic with zero revive candidates is selected as a play_card move AND a useful non-Medic line exists (score >= 25) AND no tactical exception applies, a -260 delay penalty is applied to the Medic's score.
- **Round-investment analysis:** Medic scores are adjusted (+260) when computing `positiveUnitSourceCardIds` to prevent the timing penalty from distorting round-investment decisions for non-Medic plays.
- **Emergency Medic exception:** A Medic play that clearly puts the AI ahead when behind is allowed even if it leaves no positive unit moves (Medic-specific, not generic).
- **Six exceptions** to the delay penalty: only play, last-gem catch-up, match-winning, round-3 low hand, opponent passed, and no useful non-Medic line.

## Behavior NOT Changed

- Engine rules, legal move generation, command execution
- Prompt UI, product routes, catalog data, deck presets
- Benchmark suite definitions, v0 behavior
- Medic prompt-based target ranking
- Strong revive-target Medic behavior

## Risks / Follow-ups

1. **Penalty magnitude calibration:** `MEDIC_NO_TARGET_DELAY_PENALTY = 260` is a new constant. If future scenarios show Medic being too aggressively delayed or not delayed enough, this value may need tuning.
2. **Medic score adjustment in round-investment:** Adding +260 to Medic's score for `positiveUnitSourceCardIds` computation is a targeted fix to prevent the timing penalty from indirectly affecting non-Medic play decisions. This coupling should be documented.
3. **Failure mining top queue:** `medic_no_target_timing` now appears in the failure mining top queue (3rd behind `round_resource_exhaustion` and `weathered_row_low_tempo`). This is expected as the new analysis is active. Monitor for false positives.
