# cCp32.2 Report - Scoia'tael Mulligan Presentation Repair

## Summary

cCp32.2 repairs a product presentation regression introduced by the cCp32.1 post-mulligan Scoia'tael first-player prompt. Exact-one Scoia'tael matches now preserve the AI mulligan exit animation before the first-player prompt resolves. Product diagnostics also now slice command events from the command-covered suffix of the event log, so pre-command startup events no longer shift `commandEventSummaries`.

The reported diagnostic for `ep4-mp8l0sfv` shows the AI selected a zero-card mulligan (`selectedCardCount: 0`, `keep_hand`). The missing animation was therefore a presentation regression, not evidence that the AI secretly redrew. The misleading `card_moved` attribution in `commandEventSummaries` was a diagnostic slicing bug.
