## Pre-Game Setup Behavior

Pre-game defaults to `current-northern-realms` for the human seat and
`current-nilfgaard` for the AI seat. Choosing Nilfgaard for the human deck
suggests the other current preset as opponent. Begin Match passes a serializable
setup config into `AuthenticMatchScreen` through local React state, so normal
play does not require URL edits.

## Catalog Preset Mapping Notes

`preGameViewModel.ts` maps `currentDeckPresets` through
`resolveCatalogDeckPreset`, `currentCatalogCards`, and `currentCatalogLeaders`.
A preset is ready only when its cards and leader resolve. cEp4 supports both
current presets and does not invent unavailable prototype factions.

## Match Configuration / startEngineMatch Notes

`StartEngineMatchOptions` now accepts explicit human and AI deck preset IDs while
remaining backward compatible. `startEngineMatch()` without options still starts
the old Northern Realms vs Nilfgaard default. Explicit starts keep `seat_a` as
human, `seat_b` as AI, derive factions from selected presets, and preserve debug
player IDs `human` and `ai`.

## Disabled Modes And Formats

Enabled:

- Human vs AI with `legal-heuristic-v0`.
- Standard two-gem match flow.

Disabled as future work:

- Local PvP, AI-vs-AI product mode, ranked, Bo1, and training.

## Seed Handling

The pre-game screen reads `seed` from the URL. Users can edit it, copy nonblank
seeds through `navigator.clipboard`, or leave it blank. Blank seeds are converted
to a visible generated seed before dispatching `startEngineMatch`, and the match
top bar shows the active seed and policy.

## Leader Display Repair

Added `AuthenticLeaderCard` for score cards and pre-game deck options. It uses
the shared card dimensions, renders leader images with `object-fit: contain`,
omits unit-card strength/row/ability badges, and has a deterministic
faction-styled fallback. Browser smoke verifies at least one leader image uses
`object-fit: contain`. No missing leader images were observed for the two
current pre-game presets during smoke.

## Mulligan Boundary

Mulligan remains in the match screen as an engine phase. Pre-game does not add a
new mulligan route, bypass legal `choose_mulligan` moves, or introduce free
mulligan/undo behavior.

## Public / Hidden Zone Review

Pre-game can show preset composition because no hidden match zones exist yet.
After Begin Match, AI hand and deck remain count/back only. Opponent preset name
is configuration context only; ordered deck contents and drawn AI hand
identities are not rendered. Browser smoke checks visible text for raw runtime
ids and debug field names after transition.
