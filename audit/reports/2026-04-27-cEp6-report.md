## Behavior Changed

- `AuthenticMatchScreen` now renders `AuthenticMulliganScreen` whenever the engine match is in `phase === "mulligan"`. After human confirmation and the AI `legal-heuristic-v0` mulligan command complete, the screen stays mounted for a hidden-safe AI mulligan presentation and waits for player confirmation in the handoff `Start the match?` modal before the match table appears.
- The mulligan screen renders the human opening hand from `selectEngineHumanHand`, AI hand only as backs/count, deck/discard counts from selectors, leader/faction context, visible seed, and policy label.
- The mulligan screen uses medium authentic cards for the human hand and medium leader cards for both seats. The opponent hidden hand now renders one medium back per hidden card plus the restored count badge instead of a five-card preview.
- Mulligan now resolves one card at a time, up to two redraw commands per seat. A zero-card `ChooseMulligan` keeps the current hand and ends that seat's mulligan; a one-card redraw swaps in the replacement immediately and leaves the seat in mulligan until the player keeps or uses the second redraw. The replacement card from the first redraw is a legal target for the second redraw.
- The human and AI redraw presentations animate hidden/card slots based on applied `ChooseMulligan` commands. Zero-card mulligans stay static for now; one-card redraws slide the selected card out and slide the replacement into that slot. The animation only delays follow-up UI actions; rule transitions still complete immediately through engine commands.
- The human redraw presentation keeps card opacity stable while interactions are locked. Mulligan hand cards no longer dim during first or second redraw animation, the outgoing card no longer combines `is-dimmed` opacity with the slide-out fade, and the mulligan hand strips hide scrollbars/vertical overflow while cards slide.
- After the player finishes mulligan, the mulligan surface shows an `AI choosing mulligans` indicator for a short presentation buffer before dispatching AI mulligan commands. The AI exit presentation now aggregates the latest sequential AI redraw batch, so `debugAiMulliganCount=2` animates two hidden replacement slots instead of only the latest one.
- AI presentation plays a hidden-back wave for zero-card keeps. AI redraw presentation is slower and staggered one card at a time, with the pre-AI hand frozen during sequential AI choices, selected cards removed from their original frozen slots before occupying the right-side swap slots, drawn card IDs fed directly into the reveal layers, lighter hidden-card overlap, right-anchored selected slots that push held backs left, and static backs restored after the animation finishes. This polished presentation now runs in the default non-debug mulligan UI with backs/counts only; after the animation/review completes, the mulligan screen opens the handoff `Start the match?` wax-seal modal instead of automatically switching to the match table. The modal's `review hand` action dismisses the dialog and leaves a footer `start match` action that reopens the same modal rather than entering the match directly.
- Added an explicit temporary debug path for animation inspection: `debugAiMulligan=1` reveals AI hand cards and AI mulligan decision text on the mulligan screen. `debugAiMulliganCount=0` forces a legal zero-card AI keep, `debugAiMulliganCount=1` forces a legal one-card AI redraw, and `debugAiMulliganCount=2` spends both AI redraws sequentially.
- Slowed the one-card AI replacement animation and kept the opponent hand strip from exposing vertical scrollbars while cards slide out and in.
- Human card selection is capped at one card per redraw command. Clicking a selected card deselects it and frees the slot; clicking another unselected card while one is selected is ignored until the current selection is cleared or confirmed.
- Confirming mulligan uses `findLegalMulliganMove` to match selected card instance IDs to an engine `choose_mulligan` legal move. The UI dispatches `ChooseMulligan` only from that move, including the zero-card keep-hand move.
- The match table action rail no longer contains the old mulligan confirmation block. Normal match play starts after the engine leaves mulligan.
- Match top navigation now uses `rematch` and `setup`. `rematch` restarts the same setup and seed and returns to the mulligan screen.
- Returning to setup from an in-progress mulligan or match opens the handoff `Return to setup?` wax-seal modal. Confirming return clears the engine adapter state before showing pre-game, so starting the same setup/seed again creates a fresh mulligan instead of rendering stale prior AI mulligan history. Returning to setup after game end does not require the abandon warning.
- Handoff modal shells now explicitly stay transparent while retaining the dim scrim, so `Start the match?` and `Return to setup?` render as popups over the active mulligan or match screen instead of appearing as separate parchment screens.
- Added a shared authentic button contract in `src/styles/authentic-buttons.css` plus the reusable `AuthenticButton` component, and documented the audit/variant rules in `docs/ui/authentic-button-style-guide.md`. Normal and debug authentic routes now use shared variants for primary, secondary, ghost, destructive, tile, choice, field, and icon buttons; alerts, toasts, handoff modals, and the component foundation page consume `AuthenticButton` directly; spatial card/board/pile buttons remain explicit exceptions. Primary, secondary, ghost, destructive, and flow actions now share the natural-height seal-style font scale and padding without global height forcing. Shared standard buttons enforce lowercase labels, forward flow actions use the real right-arrow glyph `→`, and primary buttons keep orange fill with white text by default, then black border, transparent background, and orange text on hover.
- Ledger and seal alert actions now share the same default `AuthenticButton` proportions instead of ledger actions defaulting to compact or flow-sized controls. Compact alert buttons are opt-in only, `next round →` no longer uses `authentic-button--flow`, and `authentic-button--flow` no longer widens buttons elsewhere.
- Added `/?engine=1&ui=authentic&view=ui-component-foundation`, a consolidated review surface for finalizing authentic UI primitives. It renders token chips, audited typography/font stacks, button variants and states, tile/choice controls, text fields, Listbox, catalog cards, backs, leader cards, alerts, toast launch, handoff modals, hidden-hand treatment, score-strip, board-row, and prompt-action patterns without starting or mutating an engine match.
- Audited current platform font usage into the component foundation page. The page now documents authentic `--font-body`, `--font-display`, and `--font-mono` stacks, the legacy `Inter, sans-serif` body stack, source files, current surface usage, loaded weights, and open decisions around isolating/replacing the legacy Inter stack and self-hosting production web fonts. The handoff modal now uses the shared authentic font variables instead of hardcoded inline families, and the Google Fonts import now includes the EB Garamond normal/italic 400/500/600/700 and JetBrains Mono 400/500/600/700 weights used by current CSS.
- Consolidated the authentic typography guideline after review: EB Garamond normal now carries the default body/label voice across pre-game, deck builder, mulligan, match, listbox, toast, handoff modal, leader, and foundation surfaces; `--font-display` remains for titles, card names, and standard button text; `--font-data` aliases JetBrains Mono and is reserved for seeds, debug traces, imported JSON, technical IDs, and compact numeric card badges. Shared primary/destructive buttons and modal body copy no longer use display italics, and the foundation font audit now documents the simplified platform rule.
- Match-end navigation labels are now deterministic: `setup` plus `rematch` when setup return exists, or `close` plus `rematch` otherwise.

## Hidden-Info Review

The default mulligan screen does not render AI hand card names, source IDs, or instance IDs. AI hand display is backs plus count only, including during the AI mulligan presentation. Unit coverage verifies AI seat summaries drop hidden hand identity data, and browser smoke scans mulligan, AI presentation, and post-confirm match text for `instanceId`, `sourceId`, and runtime seat-card ID patterns.

The debug query params intentionally reveal AI hand cards and decision text for animation debugging. They are treated as a temporary explicit debug exception, not the default human product view.

## Deviations

Engine mulligan behavior changed after user correction: redraw is now one card at a time up to two redraws, and the replacement from the first redraw can be chosen for the second redraw. No card content, AI policy, deck validation, or route promotion was changed. Rematch preserves the existing deterministic behavior by reusing the same setup and seed.

The AI mulligan animation uses presentation timers after the applied AI command. This does not drive or delay rule resolution; it only delays enabling the `start match` confirmation and prevents the immediate UI AI loop from dispatching the next gameplay command until the presentation completes and the player acknowledges it.

The only browser-smoke deviation is match-end navigation: it is helper-tested instead of browser-smoked because reaching game end through UI play would make the smoke suite long and brittle.
