## Behavior Changed

No gameplay behavior changed. cFp70 adds benchmark-only availability probe
infrastructure:

- reads committed cFp68 eligible and skipped contract artifacts;
- reads committed cFp69 public-action scaffold and skipped artifacts;
- re-observes matching benchmark roots through the existing headless root
  observer;
- regenerates cFp61 hidden multiset samples in memory through the existing
  sampler path;
- rebuilds sampled root legal-action surfaces in memory without mutating
  benchmark roots;
- collapses sampled legal moves through the existing public action abstraction;
- compares sampled public buckets against the cFp69 root public buckets;
- emits one hidden-info-safe `availability-roots.jsonl` record per eligible
  root;
- carries cFp68/cFp69 skipped roots into cFp70 `skipped-roots.jsonl` and skip
  counters.

No rollout, action evaluation, action ranking, move selection, strength claim,
product AI policy, sampler behavior change, engine rule change, legal-move
change, benchmark suite definition change, rating change, UI gameplay change,
catalog/deck change, product difficulty change, or browser benchmark execution
was added.
