## Behavior Changed

No gameplay behavior changed. cFp63 is benchmark sampler repair infrastructure:

- Public-known sampler subtraction now counts each public card instance at most once across public zones/lists.
- Prompt-revealed opponent hand cards still count once as fixed known hand cards while the materializer receives the real opponent hand count.
- Duplicate public-reference and duplicate fixed-known-hand-reference diagnostics are serialized only as scalar counts and aggregate stats.
- cFp61 sampler-materialization and cFp62 invalid-root artifacts were regenerated for the current and robust starter suites.

No PIMC, ISMCTS, MCTS, rollout search, action evaluation, action ranking,
search move selection, product AI wiring, AI policy behavior, engine rules,
legal moves, benchmark suite definitions, benchmark match records,
failure-mining classifiers/artifacts, rating artifacts, UI gameplay,
catalog/deck data, product difficulty, or browser benchmark execution changed.

## Deviations

The cFp63 repair did not convert any committed invalid roots to valid roots.
Duplicate public-reference totals and duplicate fixed-known-hand-reference
totals are zero in both current and robust artifacts, so the cFp62
`public_zone_count_deficit` roots remain unchanged. This is documented as a
falsified duplicate-reference hypothesis, not a relaxed sampler outcome.

## Risks And Follow-Ups

- The remaining invalid roots are still public-zone count deficits and must be
  skipped by any future determinized probe unless a later repair makes them
  valid.
- The next phase needs scalar-only public-zone provenance evidence because
  duplicate-reference aggregation did not explain the deficits.
- Future search work must still report invalid-root skip accounting before any
  outcome or action-value readout.
