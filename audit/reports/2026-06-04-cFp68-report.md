## Behavior Changed

No gameplay behavior changed. cFp68 adds benchmark-only contract infrastructure:

- reads committed cFp61 sampler-materialization roots and cFp67 invalid-root
  casebook records;
- emits exactly one contract record per cFp61 root, either
  `eligible_valid_root` or `skipped_invalid_root`;
- reports skipped-root counts, percentages, source consistency, skip counters,
  and eligible-root determinization budget;
- defines the future probe requirement to consume both eligible and skipped
  artifacts before reporting any result.

selection, product AI behavior, sampler behavior, engine rules, legal moves,
benchmark suite definitions, benchmark match records, rating formulas/artifacts,

benchmark execution changed.
