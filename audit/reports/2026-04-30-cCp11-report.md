## 3. Behavior Changed

- **Hero-source Morale Boost.** Heroes printed with `morale_boost` now contribute +1 to other non-hero units on their row. Heroes still cannot receive +1 (immune as receivers), and hero Tight Bond is intentionally not implemented as a functional rule.
- **Row-scoped unit Scorch.** Cards with `scorch_close`, `scorch_range`, or `scorch_siege` play onto their legal row, then check the opponent's matching opposite row's effective strength; if ≥ 10, all tied highest non-hero units on that opposite row are destroyed. Below threshold or with no eligible targets, the source remains on its row.
- **Unit-source whole-board Scorch.** Cards with `scorch` and `kind !== "special"` (Clan Dimun Pirate) play onto their legal row, then resolve a global Special-Scorch-equivalent pass. The source is included as a target — if it is the unique highest or tied for highest, it destroys itself; otherwise it remains on board. Special Scorch (the `kind === "special"` variant) keeps its existing behavior of discarding itself after firing.
- **Avenger battlefield removal replacement.** Cow and Kambi summon their linked side-deck replacement when removed from the battlefield via:
  - round cleanup (replacement is summoned **after** the row sweep, so it persists into the next round; round-end scoring/gem decisions are based on the pre-sweep board);
  - active-round Special or Unit Scorch (replacement is summoned immediately after the source moves to discard);
  - active-round Decoy (replacement is summoned on the source's old row immediately after the source returns to hand).
  Missing `linkedSourceIds[0]` or a missing side-deck replacement emits a structured `ability_resolved` event with outcome `missing_link` or `missing_replacement`, and never crashes.
- **Clear Weather (card and leader)** explicitly covers Skellige Storm. The implementation already iterated all weather entries; cCp11 adds two regression tests for this behavior.

## 8. Deviations From Spec

- **Spec §1 docs scope.** The spec asked for clarifications "where current text conflicts with user-confirmed behavior or lacks a needed implementation detail." The §17.21 rewrite changes a previously printed FAQ-derived rule (Clan Dimun Pirate's self-protection) to its user-confirmed inverse. This is a substantive rule change rather than a clarification, but it follows the user-confirmed decision in the spec and supersedes the prior FAQ reading; no backwards-compatibility shim was added per §0.
- **Spec §6 "Special Scorch is not considered a unit and is never a Scorch target".** Already satisfied because `findSpecialScorchTargets` filters by `eligibleForScorch = isUnit && !isHero`; specials have `kind === "special"` and are therefore neither units nor heroes, so they are not eligible. No code change was needed.

## 9. Risks / Follow-ups

- **Avenger replacement does not fire play-time abilities.** The replacement enters the board via the `avenger_summon` movement reason, which does not call `resolveCardAbilities`. For Bovine Defense Force (`abilities: ["none"]`) and Hemdall (`abilities: ["none"]`) this is moot. If future Avenger replacements declare on-play abilities, the spec needs to clarify whether they should fire.
- **Tight Bond / Commander's Horn hero sources.** The spec only mandated hero-source Morale Boost, but the cCp11 implementation also generalized horn-source detection to accept hero kind so future hero-printed Commander's Horn cards would emit. No hero in the current catalog has these abilities. Hero Tight Bond is intentionally not functional.
- **Decoy of opponent's Spy Avenger source.** A Spy on the opponent's side that is also an Avenger (theoretical — none exist in catalog) would be returned to the Decoy player's hand, then Avenger would summon onto the Decoy player's side at the Spy's previous board-row seat. This follows the spec's "lands on the same board side and row" rule but is untested for the cross-side case.
- **Card Studio guard for custom Avenger sources.** Card Studio does not yet block playable custom Avenger cards with empty/unresolved `linkedSourceIds[0]`. cCp9 added an analogous guard for Berserker; an extension for Avenger is a small follow-up.
