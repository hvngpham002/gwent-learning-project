# Cluster D Phase 4 Report - Engine UI Leader, Prompt, and Special Action Polish

## Summary

Implemented legal-move-backed leader controls and prompt controls in the opt-in engine shell. The legacy UI remains the default route, and the engine shell still renders only through `?engine=1` or `VITE_ENGINE_UI=1`.

The UI now derives human `UseLeader` and `ChoosePromptOption` actions from `selectEngineLegalMovesForHuman`, dispatches exact command payloads from those legal moves, and keeps ordinary play/pass/leader controls inactive while a human prompt is pending. The core legal move generator no longer advertises placeholder/planned leader abilities as executable `use_leader` moves.

Strategic AI, AI card play, placeholder leader implementations, and final spatial board UX remain out of scope.

## Files Changed

- `src/game/core/legalMoves.ts`: filters executable leader moves to implemented Clear Weather only.
- `src/components/game/EngineGameManager.tsx`: adds leader action panel, exact `UseLeader` dispatch, legal prompt-option dispatch, prompt source readability, and grouped controls.
- `src/components/game/engine/playMoveHelpers.ts`: adds helpers for leader moves, prompt moves, leader labels, leader disabled state, and prompt view models.
- `src/store/selectors/engineSelectors.ts`: enriches leader status with ability name/status metadata for display.
- `src/styles/components/engine-game.css`: improves action-panel grouping and wrapping.
- `tests/components/playMoveHelpers.test.ts`: covers leader and prompt helper behavior.
- `tests/game/legalMoves.test.ts`: covers placeholder leader omission from executable moves.
- `tests/store/engineAdapter.test.ts`: covers Redux `UseLeader` Clear Weather and prompt choice flows.

## Leader UI Behavior

- The engine shell shows a compact Leader panel during `playing`.
- The Use Leader button is enabled only when human legal moves include `kind === "use_leader"`.
- Clicking it dispatches:
  - `type: "UseLeader"`
  - `seatId: humanSeat`
  - `target: leaderMove.target`
- The target object is not reconstructed in React.
- The label includes leader name plus ability/status from the legal move or engine leader status.
- Leader readiness comes from engine state (`leaderUsed`) and legal moves.

## Legal Move / Command Consistency

- `getLegalMoves` now omits non-`clear_weather` leaders and non-implemented leader abilities from executable `use_leader` moves.
- Direct `UseLeader` commands for unsupported leaders still reject through command validation and do not consume leader use.
- Current executable leader scope remains Clear Weather.

## Prompt UI Behavior

- Prompt buttons are derived from legal `choose_prompt_option` moves for the human seat.
- Clicking a prompt button dispatches exact `seatId`, `promptId`, and `optionId` from the legal move.
- Prompt display includes prompt kind, ability, source card when visible through engine view data, and legal option labels.
- While a prompt is pending, ordinary play/pass/leader controls are inactive because the legal move list only exposes prompt choices.
- AI-owned prompts are not exposed as hidden AI hand details; the scripted preview AI remains unable to resolve non-trivial AI prompts in this phase.
